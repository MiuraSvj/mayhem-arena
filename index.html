      Rules reference

      How to battle

      Goal

      Be the last player with hit points remaining. Each character begins at 10 HP.

      Turn

      Draw one card, then play at least one. A lightning symbol adds another required card play. When your hand becomes empty, draw two cards.

      Attack ⚔

      Choose an opponent. Damage removes active defense first, then their hit points.

      Defense 🛡

      Defense stays on your board until its shields are gone. It absorbs damage before your HP.

      Other symbols

      ♥ restores HP up to 10. ▣ draws cards immediately. Every symbol on a card resolves: ⚔ + 🛡 deals damage, then stays as a defense. Non-defense cards go to your discard pile.

      Deck refresh

      When a deck runs out, its discard pile is shuffled back into the draw pile.

      This is an original fan-made implementation of the supplied game system for local play. It uses original card names and presentation.
